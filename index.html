📈 Polymarket Copy-Trade Backtester

Pick a trader's wallet, a look-back window and a bankroll. The tool replays their real trades, copies each one at your chosen size — a ratio of his, or a flat $ per market — applies fees + copy-lag slippage, exits when they exit, and tells you whether copying them would have made money.

Find it on the trader's Polymarket profile URL (polymarket.com/profile/0x…).
Share of his size you copy. 0.1 = 10% of every trade.
Same amount on every market, whatever he bets.
One budget per market — "who wins?" and "first scorer?" in the same match each get their own $1. Yes and No on the same market share it. Extra buys are skipped until he sells; any sell re-arms the full amount.
Adverse price move because you copy after he fills.
Real Polymarket taker fee: shares × rate × price × (1−price), charged on buys and sells. Settlement is free.
